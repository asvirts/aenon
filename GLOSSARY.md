# Aenon

The language of the single-player game set in the Galilee and Jordan region during one season of Jesus' ministry.

## The world

**Season**:
The single playable stretch of about 27–29 AD, with John still baptizing at Aenon and the region's episodes present together.
_Avoid_: campaign, chapter, timeline

**Settlement**:
A lived place with people, quests, scripture objects, vendors, and a level band.
_Avoid_: zone, map node

**Wilderness**:
Traversable land that is not a settlement: lake, river, hills, mountains, and valleys.
_Avoid_: overworld, path

**Spot**:
A fixed place where a gathering skill other than Hunting is practiced, with a finite supply that replenishes during active play.
_Avoid_: node, resource

**Level band**:
A place's rank by distance from Aenon. Quests, enemies, and goods there share it, and a higher band is stronger.
_Avoid_: zone level, difficulty tier, tier

## People

**Adventurer**:
The person the player controls. A normal person with no office in the Gospel, who begins in Aenon.
_Avoid_: hero, disciple, chosen one

**Vendor**:
A person in a settlement who buys goods and sells supplies and gear.
_Avoid_: merchant, shopkeeper

**Biblical figure**:
John, Jesus, someone in the traveling company, or another person the Gospels place in an episode. An ordinary person is not a biblical figure.
_Avoid_: NPC, quest giver

**Traveling company**:
The Twelve, Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Susanna, who are with Jesus.
_Avoid_: party, entourage

## Encounters

**Set scene**:
An encounter with a biblical figure whose outcome is the one the Gospel tells. It plays once in the Season, and only as a quest's attend step.
_Avoid_: cutscene, quest giver

**Acquaintance**:
A wilderness meeting with Jesus and the traveling company, marked by one encouraging line from him.
_Avoid_: set scene, quest

**Quest**:
Work given by one ordinary person in a settlement, made of ordered steps in that settlement's level band. It is once or repeatable.
_Avoid_: mission, campaign, side quest

**Quest journal**:
The Adventurer's list of started quests, each with one current line.
_Avoid_: reference journal, quest log

**Adventurer level**:
The level shared by the four combat skills. The level bands are successive spans of it. It rises from once quests, defeats, and a smaller grant for the other eleven skills, inside the place's band, except a first Scripture reading, which pays at any level.
_Avoid_: skill, skill level, level band, total level

**Combat skill**:
Attack, Strength, Defence, or Hitpoints. Each is the Adventurer level.
_Avoid_: stance, combat style

**Weapon technique**:
A player-triggered melee action with a cooldown, available alongside automatic basic attacks. It is distinct from a combat skill and a legendary piece's special effect.
_Avoid_: spell, combat skill, special effect

**Hostile**:
An animal or bandit encountered as an opponent in the wilderness. An ordinary hostile can return after defeat, while a story-specific encounter retains its authored outcome.
_Avoid_: biblical figure

**Camp**:
A few ordinary hostiles gathered at one wilderness landmark.
_Avoid_: pack, patrol, spawn

**Cache**:
A chest in the wilderness that the Adventurer can open once in the Season.
_Avoid_: secret, treasure

**Fight**:
The Adventurer's active melee danger from engaged or pursuing hostiles. It ends when every threat has fallen or disengaged, or the Adventurer is defeated.
_Avoid_: paired exchange, selected target

**Current Hitpoints**:
The Adventurer's present pool of Hitpoints, bounded by the Hitpoints skill.
_Avoid_: health, HP, life

**Scripture object**:
A period object or person tied to an NIV passage the Adventurer can read.
_Avoid_: Bible item, bookcase, lore pickup

**Reference journal**:
The filed NIV passages the Adventurer has read.
_Avoid_: Quest journal, Bible

## Goods

**Gear**:
A piece worn in a gear slot, with a rarity and a level band.
_Avoid_: equipment, loadout, item

**Gear slot**:
Weapon, shield, head, body, legs, or feet.
_Avoid_: paperdoll, equipment slot

**Weapon kind**:
Sword or mace, each one-handed or two-handed.
_Avoid_: weapon class

**Armor weight**:
Heavy, medium, or cloth, worn on head, body, legs, and feet.
_Avoid_: armor class, tier

**Special effect**:
The extra a legendary piece carries beyond its Strength or Defence.
_Avoid_: proc, enchantment, affix, meal buff

**Rarity**:
One of five grades of gear, a special find, fish, or food: common (white), uncommon (green), rare (blue), epic (purple), legendary (orange).
_Avoid_: quality, tier

**Special find**:
A legendary material of one level band, distinct from fish and from gear.
_Avoid_: token, reagent

**Everyday good**:
A tradable good with no rarity.
_Avoid_: common, gear, white item

**Fish**:
A catch with a rarity.
_Avoid_: food

**Food**:
A meal made from fish, meat, and herbs, carrying the fish's rarity. A legendary meal also carries a meal buff.
_Avoid_: potion, ration

**Meal buff**:
The temporary extra a legendary meal grants when eaten: added Defence the fight reads, or a faster walk.
_Avoid_: potion, special effect

**Recipe**:
A known pattern for one piece of gear or one food.
_Avoid_: blueprint, scroll

## Skills

**Skill**:
One of the fifteen disciplines: Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Herbalism, Harvesting, Hunting, Scripture, Blacksmithing, Tailoring, Leatherworking, and Cooking. The four combat skills are the Adventurer level. The other eleven rise by doing the work.
_Avoid_: profession, stat, Crafting, Smithing

**Skill level**:
The level of one skill. A combat skill's level is the Adventurer level. Any other skill's level is how far the Adventurer has trained it.
_Avoid_: level band

**Hunting**:
The gather of meat, hide, and bone from a wild animal.
_Avoid_: Leatherworking

**Leatherworking**:
The hide-and-bone gear skill.
_Avoid_: Hunting, Crafting

**Blacksmithing**:
The ore-and-wood gear skill.
_Avoid_: Smithing, Crafting

**Tailoring**:
The cloth gear skill.
_Avoid_: Crafting

**Herbalism**:
The herb gather.
_Avoid_: Harvesting

**Harvesting**:
The fiber gather, such as flax.
_Avoid_: Herbalism, Woodcutting
