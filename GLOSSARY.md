# Aenon

The language of the single-player game set in the Galilee and Jordan region during one season of Jesus' ministry.

## The world

**Season**:
The single playable stretch of about 27–29 AD, with John still baptizing at Aenon and the region's episodes present together.
_Avoid_: campaign, chapter, timeline

**Settlement**:
A lived place with people, quests, scripture objects, vendors, and a level band.
_Avoid_: zone, map node

**Wilderness**:
Traversable land that is not a settlement: lake, river, hills, mountains, and valleys.
_Avoid_: overworld, path

**Level band**:
A place's rank by distance from Aenon. Quests, enemies, and goods in that place share it.
_Avoid_: zone level, difficulty tier

## People

**Adventurer**:
The person the player controls. A normal person with no office in the Gospel, who begins in Aenon.
_Avoid_: hero, disciple, chosen one

**Vendor**:
A person in a settlement who buys goods and sells supplies and gear.
_Avoid_: merchant, shopkeeper

## Encounters

**Set scene**:
An encounter with a biblical figure whose outcome is the one the Gospel tells.
_Avoid_: cutscene, quest giver

**Scripture object**:
A period object or person tied to an NIV passage the Adventurer can read.
_Avoid_: Bible item, bookcase, lore pickup

## Goods

**Rarity**:
One of five grades of gear or a special find: common (white), uncommon (green), rare (blue), epic (purple), legendary (orange).
_Avoid_: quality, tier

## Skills

**Skill**:
A discipline the Adventurer trains by doing it. The skills are Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Herbalism, Harvesting, Hunting, Scripture, Blacksmithing, Tailoring, Leatherworking, and Cooking.
_Avoid_: profession, stat, Crafting, Smithing

**Hunting**:
The gather of meat, hide, and bone from a wild animal.
_Avoid_: Leatherworking

**Leatherworking**:
The hide-and-bone gear skill.
_Avoid_: Hunting, Crafting

**Blacksmithing**:
The ore-and-wood gear skill.
_Avoid_: Smithing, Crafting

**Tailoring**:
The cloth gear skill.
_Avoid_: Crafting

**Herbalism**:
The herb gather.
_Avoid_: Harvesting

**Harvesting**:
The fiber gather, such as flax.
_Avoid_: Herbalism, Woodcutting
