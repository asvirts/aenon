# One season on the map

The Gospels do not place every famous episode on this map in the same months as John's baptism at Aenon. The game lays them on one map anyway, so the region is explorable in a single Season, about 27–29 AD, with John still at Aenon. Play does not continue into the arrest or the cross.

A clock that replaced earlier events with later ones, or a world that stopped at Aenon, would have kept the chronology and cost the region.

## Considered options

- A moving clock, with later events replacing earlier ones.
- Aenon alone, and the other labels as names at the edge of the map.
