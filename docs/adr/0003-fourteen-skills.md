# Fourteen skills

The Adventurer trains fourteen skills on one list: Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Herbalism, Harvesting, Hunting, Scripture, Blacksmithing, Tailoring, Leatherworking, and Cooking. A fight trains the combat skills. Hunting then takes meat, hide, and bone. Leatherworking turns that hide and bone into gear. Blacksmithing uses ore and wood. Tailoring uses flax and herbs. Cooking uses fish, meat, and herbs.

One Crafting skill would have hidden which work makes cloth, metal, and hide. Making Hunting the hide-gear skill would have mixed the gather with the making. Prayer, Magic, and Ranged would have added a second way to fight, or a trained power beside a Gospel office the Adventurer does not hold.

## Considered options

- One Crafting skill for every kind of gear.
- Hunting as the skill that makes hide and bone gear.
- Prayer, Magic, or Ranged as further combat skills.
