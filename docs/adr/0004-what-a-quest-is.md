# What a quest is

A quest is an ordered course of steps from one ordinary person, worn at that settlement's level band, and finished by returning to that person. Once quests raise Adventurer level, a level beside the fourteen skills, and may also pay skill experience, coin, and gear. Repeatable quests pay coin or everyday goods. A quest may require other quests from the same settlement, one skill level, and an Adventurer level. It cannot require a quest from another settlement. A set scene plays once in the Season, and many quests may attend it.

A fetch-only quest would have had no way to lead to a set scene. A chain across settlements would have been a campaign spine. Paying experience only into a skill would have left no level of the Adventurer's own. Repeatable gear or experience would have sat beside the skill loop as a second faucet. Replaying a set scene for every quest would have told the Gospel more than once. Adventurer level is not a fifteenth skill.

## Considered options

- Every quest is a fetch: talk, bring a count of an item, return.
- Every quest is once.
- Prerequisite chains that cross settlements.
- Quest experience paid only into one skill.
- One quest owns each set scene, and the scene replays for each quest.
- Adventurer level as a fifteenth skill.
