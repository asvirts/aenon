# What a quest is

A quest is an ordered course of steps from one ordinary person, worn at that settlement's level band, and finished by returning to that person. Once quests raise Adventurer level and may also pay skill experience, coin, and gear. They do not pay experience into Attack, Strength, Defence, or Hitpoints. Those four are the Adventurer level, recorded in `docs/adr/0009-combat-skills-are-the-adventurer-level.md`. Repeatable quests pay coin or everyday goods. A quest may require other quests from the same settlement, one skill level, and an Adventurer level. It cannot require a quest from another settlement. When a higher band's once quests are offered is recorded in `docs/adr/0007-what-a-skill-level-gates.md`. A set scene plays once in the Season, and many quests may attend it. When it plays is recorded in `docs/adr/0008-when-a-set-scene-plays.md`.

A fetch-only quest would have had no way to lead to a set scene. A chain across settlements would have been a campaign spine. Paying experience only into a skill would have left no level of the Adventurer's own. Repeatable gear or experience would have sat beside the skill loop as a second faucet. Replaying a set scene for every quest would have told the Gospel more than once. Adventurer level is not a fifteenth skill.

## Considered options

- Every quest is a fetch: talk, bring a count of an item, return.
- Every quest is once.
- Prerequisite chains that cross settlements.
- Quest experience paid only into one skill.
- One quest owns each set scene, and the scene replays for each quest.
- Adventurer level as a fifteenth skill.
