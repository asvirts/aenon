# How a fight uses gear

A fight is one melee. The Attack level is accuracy, and a weapon adds Strength. A sword is more accurate and has less Strength than a mace of the same hold, level band, and rarity, and a mace has more Strength and is less accurate. That gap stays flat across rarity and band. Head, body, legs, and feet are heavy, medium, or cloth, and each piece still trades Defence against accuracy. At the same rarity, heavy has more Defence than medium, and medium more than cloth. A higher rarity of a lighter weight may exceed that. Rarity and level band both raise Strength or Defence. A high-band common piece is stronger than a low-band legendary of the same armor weight, and the lower legendary is still worn for its special effect. A legendary special effect is a second hit, a stun, a burn, or a heal on the piece, not a skill.

Accuracy stays on the Attack level. Weapon kind adds a flat bias, and armor still trades Defence against accuracy. A weapon that added Attack as well as Strength while armor left accuracy untouched would have put accuracy only on the weapon. One shared body slot would have made the three gear skills compete for a single piece. Rarity as a label, or gear of equal strength in every band, would have left distance from Aenon with nothing to raise but the hostiles. Replacing the legendary at every band step would have retired a special effect on the road from the Jordan to the lake. A legendary that was only a larger number would have given the old piece nothing to keep once a higher band's common outclassed it. The middle band is not ranked against the ends. A crafted piece takes the level band of the settlement where its station stands, and the spec sets no integers.

## Considered options

- A weapon that adds both Attack and Strength, and armor that does not change accuracy.
- One shared body slot.
- Rarity as a label that does not change power.
- Gear equally strong in every band.
- Every band step replacing the legendary below it.
- A legendary that is only a larger number.
