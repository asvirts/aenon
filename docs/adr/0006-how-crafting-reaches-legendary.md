# How crafting reaches legendary

A recipe is a known pattern for one piece of gear or one food, and making it succeeds. Gear is made at a forge, a loom, or a tanning rack, and only in a settlement of that piece's level band, from ore and wood, hide and bone, or flax and herbs. Those materials have no band. A legendary also spends one orange special find of that band, and the same piece can be found whole. Food is made at any hearth from fish, meat, and herbs, and the fish's rarity is the meal's rarity.

A recipe scroll, making from the pack, or a station that makes every band would have cut the piece's band loose from the place. Banded ore would have given the materials a second rank beside the station. Leatherworking weapons would have put the weapon slot on two skills. A failed make that spent the special find would have turned the reliable path into a gamble.

## Considered options

- Recipe scrolls, or recipes sold by a vendor.
- Making from the pack, with no station.
- A station that makes every level band.
- Banded ore, wood, flax, hide, and bone.
- Weapons made by Leatherworking.
- A failed make that spends the special find.
