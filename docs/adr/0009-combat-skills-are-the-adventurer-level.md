# Combat skills are the Adventurer level

Attack, Strength, Defence, and Hitpoints are the Adventurer level. They start at 1 and cap at 60 with it. A swing grants no Adventurer experience. Defeating a hostile grants it, and only while the Adventurer is inside that place's level-band span: a low-band defeat stops at 20, and a high-band defeat grants none until the Adventurer is in the high span. Doing one of the other eleven skills grants a smaller share than a defeat. The spec sets no amounts. A gather, a hunt, a gear recipe, and a meal pay that share only inside the place's span, and a hunt pays it in addition to the defeat. A first Scripture reading pays the same smaller share at any level, in any band, grants Scripture experience, and files the passage. Later readings grant nothing more. Those eleven skills still rise by their own experience and cap at 99. A once quest still raises Adventurer level only through its span, and does not also pay experience into the four combat skills. Repeatable quests pay no Adventurer experience. A fight still uses the four. Requiring one of them on a quest is requiring that Adventurer level.

A separate track for each combat skill would have let swings raise Attack past the quests and the defeats. A stance, or Defence trained when a hostile swings, would have added a training mode to one melee. Letting a low-band wolf, or an early trip to the lake, fill levels through 60 would have let one place finish the spans the bands are there to separate. Holding a Scripture grant until the Adventurer reached that place's span would have made an early find worth less. The four stay named, because the fight still reads accuracy, force, Defence, and Hitpoints, and each number is the Adventurer level.

## Considered options

- A landing swing trains Attack, Strength, Defence, and Hitpoints.
- A stance chooses Attack, Strength, or Defence, and a landing swing trains that skill and Hitpoints.
- Hostile swings train Defence.
- Adventurer level is only a ceiling, and swings still train the four up to it.
- Defeats, gathers, and crafts raise Adventurer level with no band span.
- A first Scripture reading pays its Adventurer grant only inside that place's span.
