# Where a biblical figure stands

John stays at Aenon for the whole Season, baptizing, before his set scene and after it. Speaking with him when no attend step for that scene is current is a short word while he baptizes. It is not the set scene, and it is not a quest. A later quest still completes an attend step at Aenon without a replay, as recorded in `docs/adr/0008-when-a-set-scene-plays.md`.

Jesus is met in the wilderness, the roads included, with the traveling company: the Twelve, Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Susanna. Matthew and Mark call one of the Twelve Thaddaeus; Luke calls him Judas son of James. The meeting is an acquaintance. He stops, speaks one encouraging line, and goes on. The company is with him, and clicking one of them opens no conversation. The acquaintance pays nothing, gives no quest, and keeps no friendship score. It happens once each time the Adventurer has left a settlement, until they enter one again. A fight holds it until every hostile threat has fallen or disengaged, as defined in [ADR 0020](0020-combat-boundaries-pause-and-recovery.md), and then it happens if this outing has not already had it and no unplayed set scene of his has a current attend step. Waking in the last settlement ends the outing, so the acquaintance does not play in town. The acquaintance's line is Matthew 11:28, read live by its USFM, `MAT.11.28`. It is not filed in the reference journal. John's short word stays unspecified.

While an attend step for an unplayed set scene of Jesus is current, he and the traveling company are at that place, and the acquaintance does not happen. A quest's attend step for a different set scene of his cannot become current until that scene has played. A quest may still be started and may sit on an earlier step. John's set scene can be current alongside one of his, and John stays at Aenon. A later quest completes an attend step at a scene that has already played, without a replay and without bringing the company back.

Mary the mother of Jesus is at the wedding at Cana and the short Capernaum stay when those set scenes play. She is not in the traveling company. People of one episode, such as the widow of Nain, the demoniac, and Jesus' brothers at that Capernaum stay, are at that place only while its unplayed set scene has a current attend step. A joining scene, such as Peter's call, still plays later as the Gospel tells it, though that disciple is already in the company. Holding him out until the call had played would have been a moving clock, which this Season rejects, as recorded in `docs/adr/0001-one-season-on-the-map.md`.

Parking every biblical figure at the episode place would have shown the wedding, and the widow, before a quest sent the Adventurer, and would have put Jesus in several towns at once. Leaving him alone on the road would have hidden the people Luke says traveled with him. A second current scene of his would have split one company across two places. An acquaintance during a fight would have put a conversation inside the melee.

## Considered options

- Biblical figures wait at the scene's place before the attend step and remain after the scene has played.
- Jesus is met alone.
- A rotating few of the company, or the unnamed many others of Luke 8.
- Mary the mother of Jesus travels in the company.
- A disciple joins the company only after his call scene has played.
- Each person in the company speaks at the acquaintance.
- Two unplayed set scenes of his can have a current attend step at once.
- The acquaintance begins during a fight.
