# Renewable wilderness encounters and gathering

Clicking a Spot walks into range and begins repeated timed gathering cycles, with goods and experience awarded only after each completed cycle; finite stock replenishes during active play, but depletion ends the action rather than automatically harvesting future replenishment. Ordinary wilderness animals and bandits return after a respawn delay so combat and Hunting remain repeatable, while completed quests and story-specific outcomes remain permanent. This deliberately replaces inexhaustible instant gathering and Season-long ordinary hostile defeats; live supply and hostile availability must be distinct from permanent quest history.

Movement or a new order cancels unfinished gathering, and being hit interrupts it. Completed yields remain earned; an incomplete cycle awards nothing. Paused gameplay also pauses stock replenishment and hostile respawn clocks, as part of [ADR 0020](0020-combat-boundaries-pause-and-recovery.md).
