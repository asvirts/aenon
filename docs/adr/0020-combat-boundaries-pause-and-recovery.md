# Combat boundaries, pause, and recovery

Wilderness hostiles can notice, pursue, and attack the Adventurer, with several threats active at once; after losing pursuit they return to their territory and recover. A fight lasts until every threat falls or disengages, or the Adventurer is defeated, so food and worn-Gear changes remain unavailable throughout pursuit and combat even when a panel pauses the world. Settlements stay safe; defeat returns the Adventurer to the last settlement with full current Hitpoints, the existing small coin loss, and possessions retained, replacing the prior wake-at-zero rule to avoid stranding the player without recovery.

Opening Scripture, journals, Gear, or trading stops movement and freezes timed gameplay, including cooldowns, effects, gathering, replenishment, and respawns; closing the panel leaves the Adventurer idle. Pausing does not erase threats or bypass action restrictions: Gear can be inspected during a paused fight but changed only outside combat, and permitted deliberate panel actions remain usable outside combat.

Movement takes priority over automatic attacks; movement or a new order cancels unfinished gathering and uncommitted techniques, while being hit interrupts gathering. Only completed harvest cycles award goods and experience. Gear changes must not reset cooldowns or provide an unlimited series of fresh techniques.
