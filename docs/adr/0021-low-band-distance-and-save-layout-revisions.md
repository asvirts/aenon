# Low-band distance and save layout revisions

[Issue #42](https://github.com/asvirts/aenon/issues/42) expands Aenon, Salim, Scythopolis, Pella and the Jordan wilderness together. Extend the low band southward and eastward while retaining the northern settlements, lake and landings. Keep base walking speed at 3.2 units per second. Aim for roughly one to three minutes on representative settlement journeys, using eight reused building footprints, open streets and wilderness landmarks to make that distance readable. The short Salim leg needs more than the initial threefold spacing guideline to reach a minute. See the measured routes and playable evidence in the regional review.

The save format keeps version 3 and gains an independent world-layout revision. Revision 2 identifies this geography. Before replacing a revision-1 save, validate its progress and verify a backup of the original bytes. Return affected Adventurers to a safe settlement entry and move affected ground loot and hostile positions without changing quest identities, economy, experience or possessions. Retain current-layout positions and northern boat crossings. Refuse unsupported future revisions through the existing recovery interface. Further northern scale work is tracked separately in [issue #44](https://github.com/asvirts/aenon/issues/44).
