# Occupied wilderness routes

Wilderness roads and lake shores carry camps and off-road caches. The road center, the waterline, and the open country between routes stay clear. [Issue #42](https://github.com/asvirts/aenon/issues/42) lengthened the low-band roads and left them as scenery; those walks felt empty. Filling the interior, or letting the road itself pull a fight, would have crowded the country the longer roads were meant to show. A camp is ordinary hostiles of that ground's level band. A cache opens once. Neither one carries a level band: the settlement once-quest course still does. The placement rule and rewards are specified in [issue #46](https://github.com/asvirts/aenon/issues/46).

## Considered options

- Leave the lengthened roads as scenery.
- Fill the open country between routes as well.
- Place camps on the road so travel itself pulls them.

## Consequences

- A later scale of middle- and high-band distances recomputes camp and cache positions from the route rule. Quest hostiles and the existing caches stay where they are.
- Settlements and boats remain safe, as in [ADR 0020](0020-combat-boundaries-pause-and-recovery.md). Ordinary hostiles still respawn, as in [ADR 0019](0019-renewable-wilderness-encounters-and-gathering.md).
