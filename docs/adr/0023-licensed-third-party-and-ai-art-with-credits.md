# Licensed third-party and AI-generated art, with credits

Original-only art left the world well short of the Albion Online reference, so [issue #41](https://github.com/asvirts/aenon/issues/41) reopens [ADR 0017](0017-original-art-through-blender.md). The Season may now use third-party art and AI-generated art, including models, alongside original work. Every asset must be free, and licensed for modification and redistribution from a public repository and a public web game. That means CC0, CC-BY 3.0 or 4.0 with attribution, or output from a tool whose terms grant commercial and redistribution rights. The following are excluded:

- non-commercial, no-derivatives and share-alike licences
- store licences that forbid redistributing raw files
- paid assets, while the repository is public
- art taken from other games
- anything without a clear licence

Every shipped asset is recorded with its source, author, licence, retrieval date and changes, plus the tool and prompt for AI output. That record produces the repository's credits and a Credits screen in the game's menu.
