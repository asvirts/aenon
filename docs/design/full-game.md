# Completing the Season

The user requested finishing the game after the 3D first area and live NIV reading were working. This expands implementation beyond the earlier first-area boundary. The region and rules issues remain the source of scope, with the accepted 3D and real-time ADRs taking precedence over their older presentation and paired-combat language.

## Required playable region

All eighteen settlements must be reachable and have comparable depth: Aenon, Salim, Scythopolis, Pella, Gadara, Nazareth, Cana, Sepphoris, Gath-hepher, Nain, Legio, Jotapata, Tiberias, Magdala, Capernaum, Bethsaida, Gamla, and Hippus. The Jordan, Sea of Galilee, Kishon, Jezreel, Gennesaret, Tabor, Gilboa, and Moreh must be traversable, with connected roads, appropriate gathering, wilderness opponents, and contextual Scripture. Boats connect lake shores without a Sailing skill.

Each settlement needs its own identity, ordinary people, a local course of once quests, repeatable provision work, a Vendor, Scripture, and the four crafting stations. Settlements share their band's recipe list. Not every settlement has a Gospel event; memory passages and contextual readings must be identified honestly. Authored scene summaries are narration, not invented biblical quotations. NIV wording is fetched live and never bundled into content.
